What can sync?

Now: One remote press affects the projector and WiZ lights together. Try Nebula to step through a matching room palette; B+ and B− adjust brightness.

Next hardware step: Wire the IR emitter and transistor, then the ESP32 can send projector commands from this page and coordinate scenes without pressing the remote.

Color limit: This remote exposes mode and relative brightness/speed buttons, not exact RGB values. The WiZ colors are coordinated palettes, not a pixel-perfect projector color match.
